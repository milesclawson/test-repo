**Why this order:** Weeks 1–3 build safe lab habits, math, and equipment skills so labs can start by week 1 and ramp up. The nutrition-science core (S1) comes early while it carries the most exam weight and feeds every later lab. ## 5. Mini-unit U15 — Freeze Dryer & Almond Milk Machine (5 days)

| Day | Focus | Lab |
|---|---|---|
| 68 | How freeze-drying works (sublimation), food science, shelf life, safe operation | — |
| 69 | Load the freeze dryer (fruit, yogurt drops); almond milk machine safety and cleaning | Lab A: almond milk, then flavor variations |
| 70 | Nutrition & cost: homemade vs store-bought plant milks (label comparison, cost per serving) | Lab B: milk variations (vanilla, cocoa); freeze dryer continues |
| 71 | Rehydration, storage, packaging, food-safety limits (no raw meat/eggs, allergen cleaning) | Lab C: unload, taste test, rehydrate, label and package |
| 72 | Application: design a product, cost it, pitch it (ties to S3.4, S4.3) | Lab D: product prep and pitch |

Equipment (from the manufacturer pages you supplied):
- **Almond Cow Essentials Bundle**: the milk machine, a 60 oz glass jug and a jug brush. It makes 5–6 cups per batch, takes under a minute, and takes up to 1 cup of dry ingredients in the filter basket. It works with **any nut, seed or grain**, so the same machine makes **oat milk**. That gives you a built-in tree-nut-free track.
- **Harvest Right Home Pro Freeze Dryer Bundle**: comes in S, M, L and XL; the link doesn't say which size you have (see open questions). All sizes include a vacuum pump that runs on oil (change it every 20–30 batches), and the M size includes trays, Mylar bags, oxygen absorbers and an impulse sealer. The manufacturer's page does **not** state cycle time or which foods to avoid; I'll use the *Guide to Freeze Drying* that ships with the unit and your own first test batches for those, and won't invent numbers.

Notes: the machine is loaded Monday and unloaded Friday (cycles run many hours; confirm yours from the manual). **Tree-nut allergy plan required**: any student with a tree-nut allergy does the **oat milk** track on the same machine. Because the machine is shared, clean it thoroughly (and run the oat track first) or give that student a non-machine role if the allergy is severe. Almonds must be labeled peanut-free.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Formative (20%):** daily exit tickets, bellringers, lab station checks, guided practice sheets.
- **Summative (80%):** a Quia test per unit (cert-style multiple choice, 5–8 items per exam objective taught, mapped to blueprint percentages); lab performance rubrics; the unit project.
- **Cumulative:** two 55-item cert-style practice exams (about day 45 and day 85), weighted to the 38/16/38/8 blueprint.
- **Performance evaluation (optional on cert):** one rubric per cert standard, scored 0–10, with a target average of 8 or higher. Scoring tasks are embedded in labs: U2/U19 (scale a recipe), U1/U7 (clean, separate, cook, chill), U10 (geography/diet/budget problem), U9 (speak to a young adult about healthy eating), U18 (career-readiness self-assessment).
