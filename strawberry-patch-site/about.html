Our Story

Family-Owned Since 1979

A Minnesota State Fair tradition, now growing beyond the fairgrounds.

The Strawberry Patch started as a family-owned booth at the Minnesota State Fair back in 1979, serving up fresh strawberry shortcake and cheesecake in front of the horse barn — and that's still exactly where you'll find us today.

The business is family-run by three equal owners — Shannon, Sean, and Molly — and is proudly majority female-owned. What started as a single 12-day fair booth has grown to include a food truck, now bringing the same recipes to smaller fairs, festivals, and private events around the Twin Cities.

Everything we serve is made fresh, the recipes haven't changed much in decades, and neither has the reason we do it: it's a family tradition worth showing up for, one fair season after another.
