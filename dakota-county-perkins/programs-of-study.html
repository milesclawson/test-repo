Programs of Study

26 state-recognized and Perkins-funded Programs of Study span seven career fields across the consortium's secondary districts and Dakota County Technical College.

What makes a Program of Study "state-recognized"

A Program of Study (POS) must meet two of three quality criteria: it develops high-skilled individuals, prepares people for high-wage jobs, and/or trains for in-demand occupations, as identified by DEED and the CLNA.

Type key

S–R State-recognized   Both State-recognized & Perkins-funded   Funding Funded, in development toward full recognition

Minimum requirement

Minnesota requires each consortium to identify at least six state-recognized Programs of Study spanning at least four different career fields, with no more than one brokered from an outside consortium.

Growth

New & expanding programs, 2024–2026

Health Sciences expansion

SSD 6 launched a Health Science program in 2024–25 that is still establishing itself. ISD 192 is launching its own Health Science program for 2025–26, and Reserve funds are targeted at strengthening health sciences equipment consortium-wide given that health care is the Twin Cities' largest employing sector.

Culinary & agriculture programs

ISD 200 is adding a culinary program for 2025–26. ISD 659 relaunched its Agriculture, Food & Natural Resources program in 2024–25 and continues strengthening its culinary offerings.

ISD 917 program redesign

ISD 917 is redesigning its CTE programs to better align with member districts and to anticipate both the DCTC–Inver Hills merger and a potential Dakota County – South Metro consortium merger.

Recent secondary program approvals

Multiple districts completed five-year secondary program approvals in late 2025, including Farmington (Health Science Education; Family & Consumer Science; Trade & Industry) and West St. Paul–Mendota Heights–Eagan (Business).

Dakota County Perkins Consortium

A Minnesota Perkins V local consortium linking nine Dakota County school districts, BlueSky Online, and Dakota County Technical College to strengthen Career and Technical Education for every student.
