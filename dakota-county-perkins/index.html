Who We Are

A regional partnership, not a single program

Under Perkins V — the federal Strengthening Career and Technical Education for the 21st Century Act — Minnesota organizes CTE funding through 23 regional consortia. The Dakota County Perkins Consortium is the partnership of secondary districts and the postsecondary college that plan, fund, and report on CTE together every year.

Secondary Districts

Nine independent school districts and one online charter school design and deliver approved CTE programs of study to students in grades 9–12.

Dakota County Technical College

DCTC is the consortium's postsecondary partner, offering concurrent enrollment, contract courses, and direct pathways from high school CTE programs into college credentials.

Shared Governance

Representatives from every member meet monthly to review funding requests, align priorities, and jointly author the annual Perkins V Local Application.

FY26 Priorities

What the consortium is working on this year

Every year the consortium completes a Comprehensive Local Needs Assessment (CLNA) and builds strategies around the results. Four themes are driving current work.

Growing participation & concentrators

Secondary CTE participation dipped from 4,730 students in 2021 to 4,698 in 2024, with only 53% becoming concentrators. The consortium is expanding middle school career exploration and redesigning high school career fairs to reverse the trend.

A new CTE Connections Liaison

A shared position now bridges classrooms and local industry — coordinating guest speakers, tours, work-based learning placements, and equipment donations across every member district.

Closing equity gaps

Male CTE concentrators (38.9%) still outpace female concentrators (29.4%), and postsecondary students with disabilities, single parents, and out-of-workforce individuals lag the college average. Targeted outreach and advisory-committee diversity are priority strategies.

Preparing for a consortium merger

With DCTC and Inver Hills Community College merging, Dakota County is working alongside the South Metro Perkins Consortium to align needs assessments, advisory structures, and governance ahead of a combined consortium.
