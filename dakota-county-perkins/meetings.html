What Happens at Each Meeting

Recurring agenda items

Budget & funding requests

Every funding request is reviewed together against the current application, the CLNA, and prior decisions before approval.

Application & APR tracking

A shared per-district spreadsheet tracks approved programs, teachers, courses, and Perkins priorities to support both the annual application and the October 15 Annual Performance Report.

Programs of Study updates

Progress on state-recognized POS, industry credentials and certifications, and internship/GPS opportunities.

Advisory committee coordination

Updates from CTE Advisory Committees, including guidance on industry-standard equipment and materials.

Work-Based Learning

WBL Coordinator caseload guidance, endorsement cohorts, and professional development planning (e.g. with Lakes Country Service Cooperative).

Upcoming events

Regional opportunities such as the DCTC Open House, Manufacturing Month, and the Construct Tomorrow career expo at the Minnesota State Fairgrounds.

Funding Requests

How a funding request moves through the consortium

1. Submit

District submits a request

CTE teachers submit requests through a shared Google Form, describing the item, cost, and alignment to the local application.

2. Align

Check alignment to the application & CLNA

The Secondary Perkins Consortium Coordinator reviews the request against the current Local Application and CLNA priorities.

3. Review

Consortium discussion

Requests are reviewed together at the monthly meeting for input, timing, and any budget conflicts.

4. Approve & submit

MDE submission (if needed) & communication

Approved requests requiring state sign-off are submitted to MDE; the requesting teacher and business contact are notified of the consortium's decision.
